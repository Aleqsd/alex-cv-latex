You&#39;ll also represent GitLab in OpenBao&#39;s governance and technical discussions, helping ensure our product direction and upstream contributions reinforce each other.&lt;/p&gt; &lt;h4&gt;How we interview&lt;/h4&gt; &lt;h4&gt;What you&#39;ll do&lt;/h4&gt; &lt;ul&gt; &lt;li value=&quot;1&quot;&gt;Lead the technical strategy for GitLab Secrets Manager, setting architecture direction for secure, multi-tenant secrets management at scale.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li value=&quot;2&quot;&gt;Own the integration between GitLab and OpenBao, including namespaces, authentication mechanisms, and policy management.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li value=&quot;3&quot;&gt;Collaborate with Pipeline Security, Authentication, and Platform teams to propose, review, and deliver cross-team secrets management improvements.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li value=&quot;4&quot;&gt;Partner with GitLab.com Infrastructure teams to ensure secrets management meets reliability, performance, and operational requirements.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li value=&quot;5&quot;&gt;Represent GitLab in the OpenBao open source project by contributing features upstream, participating in technical steering discussions, and maintaining strong technical credibility.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li value=&quot;6&quot;&gt;Mentor and advise engineers on secrets management, cryptographic systems, and secure architecture patterns, raising the quality and consistency of designs and implementations.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li value=&quot;7&quot;&gt;Interface with engineering managers and senior leadership to scope initiatives, clarify tradeoffs, and unblock delivery across teams.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li value=&quot;8&quot;&gt;Engage with customers and external stakeholders to understand real-world needs and communicate GitLab&#39;s secrets management capabilities and roadmap direction.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;/ul&gt; &lt;h4&gt;What you&#39;ll bring&lt;/h4&gt; &lt;ul&gt; &lt;li value=&quot;1&quot;&gt;Experience designing and operating secrets management systems (for example, HashiCorp Vault, OpenBao, or cloud-native offerings), including secure storage, access control, and audit logging.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li value=&quot;2&quot;&gt;Ability to lead architecture decisions for resilient, multi-tenant services that handle secrets operations at scale, including high availability and cluster management patterns.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li value=&quot;3&quot;&gt;Working knowledge of cryptographic and key management concepts, such as encryption in transit and at rest, key derivation, and hardware security module (HSM) or PKCS#11 integrations.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li value=&quot;4&quot;&gt;Experience implementing authentication and authorization integrations, such as JSON Web Token (JWT) or OpenID Connect (OIDC), mutual Transport Layer Security (mTLS), and certificate-based authentication.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li value=&quot;5&quot;&gt;Proficiency building product integrations in Go (within the OpenBao or Vault ecosystem) and Ruby on Rails for GitLab platform integration.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li value=&quot;6&quot;&gt;Experience contributing to open source projects and working effectively with distributed governance, balancing upstream needs with product requirements.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li value=&quot;7&quot;&gt;Demonstrated ability to operate with high autonomy, drive strategy, and serve as a trusted partner to senior leaders (including constructively challenging assumptions and tradeoffs).&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li value=&quot;8&quot;&gt;Strong communication and collaboration skills to influence across teams and levels, including mentoring engineers and working in a fully remote, asynchronous environment.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;/ul&gt; &lt;h4&gt;About the team&lt;/h4&gt; &lt;p&gt;The Secrets Management team sits within the Pipeline Security group in GitLab&#39;s Software Supply Chain Security stage. We own GitLab Secrets Manager, an OpenBao-powered capability that helps teams securely store, distribute, and manage the lifecycle of secrets used across continuous integration and continuous delivery (CI/CD) pipelines. The team works closely with Authentication, Authorization, Compliance, and Platform counterparts to deliver secure defaults, reliable operations for GitLab.com, and product-grade integration between GitLab and OpenBao (including namespaces, authentication, and policy management).
