## FINAL RECOMMENDATION

Use the Software Engineer version as the default base resume. It has the broadest market fit, preserves the strongest engineering signals from both Sony and Jam.gg, and can be tuned upward toward Founding Engineer or sideways toward Platform Engineer faster than the other two versions.

For under-5-minute customization per application:
- Swap the headline and first two summary lines to mirror the job title and top responsibilities.
- Reorder the Skills groups so the most relevant stack appears first.
- Adjust the Jam.gg title line between `Founding Engineer` and `Founding Engineer / Product Manager` depending on whether startup/product range helps or hurts.
- For platform-heavy roles, move the Sony bullets above all else and trim Twitch or product wording.
- For startup-heavy roles, keep the Jam.gg scale metrics and first-30-hires line; for engineer-heavy roles, remove the recruiting line first.
- Add only job-description keywords that are already supported by the source resume, especially around backend, CI/CD, automation, cloud, and integrations.
